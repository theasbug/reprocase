# Community routing

Checked 11 November 2026. Recheck the destination's visible rules before each submission. The maintainer must be identified as the project's author. A draft does not imply moderator approval.

## X

The [launch video](https://x.com/theasbug/status/2120583234086228135) is published and pinned. Use the English posts as the primary series; Turkish variants support Turkish conversations. Publish one relevant item at a time and respond to substantive questions. Describe only verified behavior.

X showed a graduated-access notice on the new account: discoverability and messages to non-followers may initially be limited or filtered. Allow ordinary account use to establish the account; do not claim that researcher invitations were delivered until the UI confirms sending.

## Reddit / r/netsec

The current [November discussion/tool thread](https://www.reddit.com/r/netsec/comments/1xl4ghe/rnetsec_monthly_discussion_tool_thread/) is the destination for tool links. Rule 8 directs tool/README submissions into this thread. Rule 9 states: **LLM-generated posts are not welcome**. See the [community rules](https://www.reddit.com/r/netsec/about/rules/).

The maintainer should write a short original introduction in their own words, explaining why they built the tool, the fresh-account workflow, Windows-beta limitations and the feedback sought. Link the repository and video. An [AI-assisted reference draft](reddit-draft.en.md) was subsequently requested; it is marked unsubmitted and needs a moderator exception before use in r/netsec. Do not substitute a generated technical article as a way around the rule.

r/bugbounty has removed primarily promotional posts under its Promotions rule. Obtain a moderator answer before submitting there. A free license alone is not evidence of permission.

## Hacker101 / Bugcrowd

Find communities through [HackerOne's official starting page](https://www.hackerone.com/hackers/how-to-start-hacking) and [Bugcrowd's official server listing](https://discord.com/servers/319555028341882885). Read each server's rules and use a channel that permits projects/tool feedback. Do not treat joining a server as permission to post into arbitrary channels.

Where project introductions are explicitly permitted, this draft is available:

> Disclosure: I'm building ReproCase, an Apache-2.0 bug bounty workbench. I'm looking for five people to try a local IDOR reproduction workflow: run it, replace the accounts, rerun, then enable the secure fixture. The demo needs no API key. Windows portable beta and source: https://github.com/theasbug/reprocase. I'd value feedback on setup, identity binding and unclear result states. No private target captures are needed.

## Researcher invitation

Invite a small number of relevant researchers individually. Explain one verifiable reason their expertise fits, then ask one specific workflow question. The private contact list and delivery log are kept outside this public kit. A closed DM or request for a paid account is a reason to leave the invitation pending, not to post it repeatedly under unrelated work.

## Show HN

After real testers have tried the workflow, the maintainer can submit a human-written explanation of the runnable project. The [Show HN guidelines](https://news.ycombinator.com/showhn.html) prioritize something people can try; the [HN guidelines](https://news.ycombinator.com/newsguidelines.html) prohibit generated/AI-edited posts and automated posting. This kit supplies evidence and project facts, not a generated HN submission. Do not solicit coordinated votes.
